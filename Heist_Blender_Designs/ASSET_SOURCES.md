# Arena V0.3 — asset and tool notes

## Imported environment props

Both external models are Poly Haven assets released under CC0. Their low-resolution Blender packages and included textures are stored under `assets/polyhaven/models/` and their images are packed into `heist_district_v0_3.blend` for portability.

- [Security Camera 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/security_camera_02) — placed on the west/east interior walls. Its unused armature modifiers were removed because the props are static.
- [Industrial Caged Sconce](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_caged_sconce) — used as a pair of wall fixtures.
- V0.2 surface materials and studio environment — [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/), CC0; downloaded files are under `assets/polyhaven/`.

## Optional asset tools

- BlenderKit’s Blender add-on has been installed and enabled locally. No BlenderKit library assets were added to the scene because use of its online library needs an account/sign-in, and no authenticated session was available during this pass.
- Meshy’s Blender bridge was not used: the bridge requires a Pro-level account. Its separate website export route requires an authenticated account, and no signed-in session was available. No Meshy model or paid plan was used.

## Scope and visual status

V0.3 is a more detailed and better-lit concept pass, not a finished match for the cinematic reference. The original toy-like characters remain, and the battle / result monitor content is still a fixture preview rather than a live web application. The Blender scenes remain a visual prototype; external agent feeds and referee-verified outcomes are not connected.
